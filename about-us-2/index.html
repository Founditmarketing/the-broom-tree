Jake’s PLace

Est:2025

 

What began as a summer camp in 2022 for children with special needs has grown into Jake’s Place, an inclusive indoor playground that opened in 2025 to serve families across Central Louisiana. Created to meet a need in our community, Jake’s Place provides a year-round space where children of all abilities can play, learn, and build friendships together. By welcoming both children with disabilities and their neurotypical peers, we foster inclusion, understanding, and meaningful connections while creating a supportive community for the entire family.

Open Play

Open Play at Jake’s Place gives children of all abilities the opportunity to explore, play, and connect in a safe, inclusive environment. With sensory-friendly features, active play areas, and plenty of room for imagination and movement, kids can have fun while parents relax and build community. Open Play is just $10 per child, making it an affordable way for families to enjoy quality playtime together.

Birthday Parties

Make your child’s special day unforgettable at Jake’s Place! Our private birthday parties give you exclusive access to the entire playground, creating a fun, safe, and stress-free environment for children of all abilities to celebrate together. Party packages are $250 plus tax and include private use of the facility. A $75 non-refundable deposit is required to reserve your date, with the remaining balance due one week prior to your party. Let us help you create a celebration filled with fun, laughter, and lasting memories!
